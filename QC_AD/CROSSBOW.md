# Crossbow Knight weapon

Crossbow Knights drop a usable crossbow through the existing enemy-loot system. Each fresh pickup adds 12 bolts to a separate, hidden player reserve. Each crossbow holds only one chambered bolt and starts empty. It supports either hand, a support-hand grip, holstering, physical drops, force grabbing, saves and map transitions. Stone/statue exclusions and the global enemy-drop chance multiplier still apply.

Lower the crossbow to a hip holster (or use its reload button) to load one bolt. Press the trigger to fire, then release and reload before the next shot. The cooldown is 0.8 seconds. Bolts travel at 900 Quake units/second and deal 50 base damage, with the existing headshot multiplier. There is light recoil and firing haptics. Bolts are not recoverable and never draw from shells, nails or cells. Reloading transfers one reserve bolt into the chamber. The reserve is shared by carried crossbows and stays with the player when a weapon is dropped; its chambered bolt stays with the weapon. Re-catching the same weapon cannot grant another bundle. The single-bolt requirement also applies when ordinary weapon reloading is disabled. It cannot parry.

The model is AD's detached `progs/w_dcrossbow.mdl`, rotated and scaled for VR with its original skin and UVs preserved in an extended texture atlas for the added shaft, metal tip and fletching. It is approximately 85 cm long. Firing plays a 0.18-second bow-limb release, rebound and settling animation. The chambered bolt is visible only when loaded, including in holsters and on the ground. In that pose the string is drawn behind the bolt's nock; firing releases it to its original resting position. There is no manual bolt-insertion interaction. Model slot 27 holds the calibration, initialized by `advr_defaults`. Calibration version 3 enables the reload button and raises the support-hand anchor to Z=-0.5. Calibration version 4 aligns the projectile muzzle with the visible bolt tip on the sloped rail. Other grip adjustments are preserved.

Regenerate using `python QC_AD/tools/make_crossbow_model.py <game>/ad`. This writes `QC_AD/runtime/progs/advr_crossbow.mdl` and `Quake/vr/vr_ad_crossbow.inc`. Build the engine and QC, then install the model under `advr/progs/` alongside the updated executable and `advr/progs.dat`.

Validation: `advr_selftest crossbow` on `ad_e1m1` checks Knight loot, both hands, twelve reload/shot cycles, firing poses, impact damage, empty reserve, shared ammo with independent chambers, pickup duplication, regular-ammo isolation and old-magazine conversion. `advr_selftest crossbowsaved` verifies chambers and reserve after a real save/load and map change. `advr_selftest combat` checks bolt headshots through the native impact callback. The mock-VR crossbow test also renders held/dropped models and checks two-handed aiming. Old 12-shot crossbows keep one loaded bolt and move their remaining bolts into reserve on conversion, without creating new ammunition. Headset animation appearance still requires user testing.
