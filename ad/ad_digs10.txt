* Running ad_digs10.bsp *

Place ad_digs10.bsp and ad_digs10.lit in quake\ad181\maps\
From your quake directory type: 

quakespasm.exe -game ad181 +map ad_digs10

or start game with mode 

quakespasm.exe -game ad181

and from console type: map digs08
